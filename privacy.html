Personal information and your rights

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This notice explains how HOODKIID Apparel (“HOODKIID”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information in accordance with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). It is a practical privacy notice, not legal advice.

Responsible party and contact

HOODKIID Apparel, based in Soshanguve, South Africa, is responsible for personal information collected through this store. To ask a privacy question, exercise a right or make a complaint, contact us on WhatsApp: 076 993 4759. Please do not send passwords or payment credentials.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the store, we may process your email address, name, call and WhatsApp numbers, account sign-in identifiers, order items and status, delivery or pickup details, payment-verification status, newsletter subscription, and support messages you choose to send. A small cart may be stored in your browser. We do not collect card numbers or offer card processing.

Why and on what basis

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide customer support, fulfil and track orders, verify EFT payments, arrange delivery or pickup, respond to privacy requests, and meet legal or accounting obligations. These uses are based on steps needed to provide a service you request, our legal obligations, and legitimate interests in operating and securing the store. Newsletter messages are sent only after you choose to subscribe; you can ask us to remove your subscription.

Who may receive it

Access is limited to authorised HOODKIID team members who need the information for their work. Our online store uses Supabase for account, database and image-storage services and a website host to deliver these pages. We may share only the relevant information with delivery providers or pickup partners to fulfil an order, and with professional advisers or authorities where lawfully required. EFT payments are checked with the receiving bank; we do not send card data to a payment processor.

Storage and safeguards

We use access controls and database policies intended to restrict customer records to the account holder and authorised staff. Information is kept in systems used to operate the service and may be processed outside South Africa by service providers, subject to the safeguards and transfer requirements applicable under POPIA. No online service can promise absolute security; contact us promptly if you suspect account misuse.

Retention

We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purposes above. Account and contact information is retained while an account is active and for a reasonable period needed to handle support, security and legal matters. Order, payment and accounting records may be retained for periods required by tax, consumer or other laws even if an account is deleted. Newsletter details are removed when you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them, subject to any legal requirement.

Your POPIA rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct your information, request deletion or destruction, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is the basis, or complain to the Information Regulator. Use My profile to download an available copy of your account, profile, order and newsletter data, and to submit an account-deletion request. A deletion request is reviewed; we may need to verify your identity or retain limited records where the law requires it. We will explain any limitation and contact you about the request.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa. We encourage you to contact HOODKIID first so we can try to resolve your concern.

Children

If you are under 18, please involve a parent or legal guardian when creating an account or placing an order. We do not knowingly seek more information from children than is needed to provide the service.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our practices or legal obligations change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
